Your Privacy Choices

Last Updated: April 19, 2026

You're in control of your data in Load Ledger. This page summarizes the choices and rights available to you. For full details, see our Privacy Policy.

Access Your Data

All of the data you enter is visible to you inside the App at any time:

  • Loads and expenses: View under the Loads tab.
  • Reports and totals: View under the Reports tab.
  • Profile and account info: View and edit under the Profile tab.

Export Your Data

Pro users can export load and expense data as a CSV file from the Reports tab. The export includes every load and expense linked to your account.

Update Your Information

Open the App and go to Profile → Edit Profile to change your name, truck type, pay structure, or cost preferences. Changes take effect immediately.

Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time. To request deletion, email developer.jerrymac@gmail.com from the email address on your account. We will permanently remove your profile, loads, expenses, receipt images, and any related records from our servers within 30 days.

Opt Out of Analytics

Load Ledger uses PostHog to collect anonymous product analytics — which features are used, how often, and where errors happen. PostHog receives an anonymous user identifier and basic interaction events. It does not receive your name, email, load details, expense amounts, or any financial data.

To opt out of analytics, uninstall the App. Analytics collection stops immediately when the App is removed. You may also email us at developer.jerrymac@gmail.com to request that your existing analytics records be deleted.

Manage Your Subscription

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. To cancel, change, or view your subscription, open iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions on your device, or open the App Store app and tap your profile photo. Cancellations take effect at the end of the current billing period.

What We Do Not Do

  • We do not sell your personal data to anyone.
  • We do not share your load data, expense records, or financial information with third parties.
  • We do not use your data for advertising.
  • We do not show ads or third-party tracking pixels in the App.

Contact Us

For any privacy-related question or request, email developer.jerrymac@gmail.com. We respond to privacy requests within 30 days.
